Frequently asked questions

What creators ask
before they start

The practical details to know before creating your first source-aware ResetUI redesign.

Talk to our team →
What does ResetUI need to start?+

A publicly accessible landing-page URL and a design prompt describing the new direction.

Does ResetUI copy my existing layout?+

No. ResetUI preserves useful source content and brand context while generating a distinct visual system.

What happens to captured media?+

Approved website images and videos stay in foreground content slots and are never repurposed as hero or CTA backgrounds.

Can I edit the generated design?+

Yes. Project-aware chat targets the relevant section, and every accepted change creates a recoverable design version.

What can I export?+

Export standalone HTML/Tailwind CSS/JS or a one-file Vite React, Next.js App Router, Vue, or Svelte implementation.
