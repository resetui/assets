Your questions,
answered

The practical details to know before creating
your first source-aware redesign.

What does ResetUI need to start?

A publicly accessible landing-page URL and a design prompt describing
the visual direction you want ResetUI to explore.

Does ResetUI copy my existing layout?

No. ResetUI preserves useful source content and brand context while
generating a distinct visual system guided by your prompt.

What happens to captured media?

Approved website images and videos stay in foreground content slots
and are not repurposed as hero or CTA backgrounds.

Can I edit the generated design?

Yes. Project-aware chat targets the relevant section, and every accepted
change creates a recoverable design version.

What can I export?

Export standalone HTML with Tailwind CSS and JavaScript, or a one-file
React, Next.js, Vue, or Svelte implementation.
